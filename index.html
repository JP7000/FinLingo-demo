CREDIT FOUNDATIONS · LESSON 3

Credit: borrowing with a plan

A quick introduction to how credit works.

What is credit?

Credit lets you borrow money or access goods and services with an agreement to repay later. It can be useful, but borrowing comes with responsibilities, especially understanding interest, fees, and repayment terms.

Why does interest matter?

Interest is the cost of borrowing money. When comparing credit options, pay attention to the interest rate, fees, repayment schedule, and total amount you may owe, not just the monthly payment.

Remember: A lower monthly payment doesn’t automatically mean a loan costs less overall.
